Step one

Getting in

You edit the website in a free tool called Pages CMS. You sign in to it with your GitHub username and password, so keep those somewhere safe.

  1. Go to app.pagescms.org and click Sign in with GitHub. Sign in with your GitHub username and password.
  2. The first time only: if it says there are no repositories, click the button to install or add one, choose Only select repositories, tick casa-rossa-montalcino and click Install.
  3. Click casa-rossa-montalcino to open the website. Next time you visit, you'll go straight to it.
  4. Choose casa-rossa-montalcino to open the website.

While it's being finished, the website is at casa-rossa-montalcino.vercel.app. When it launches, it will move to casarossa.estate. Keep it open in another tab so you can check your changes.

How it works

The basics

On the left of Pages CMS is a list of the website's pages: Home page, The House page, The Area page, The Experience page, Booking page, Gallery and Contact details (footer). Click one to see everything on it you can change.

  1. Open a page and make your change in the box.
  2. Click Save (top right).
  3. Wait about a minute, then refresh the website to see it live.

Always press Save. Nothing changes on the website until you do, and this includes dragging photos into a new order.

Sections are folded up to keep things tidy. Click a section's name to open it, and click again to fold it away.

Nothing is ever lost. Every version you save is kept. If something goes wrong, Hillary can bring back any earlier version of the website.

Text

Changing the words

Click into any box and type. Most pages have a Small heading (the little capital letters above a title), a Heading and some Text.

Simple formatting

You can style words with a few marks. Type them exactly as shown:

You typeVisitors see
*podere*podere (italic)
**ten guests**ten guests (bold)
[see the gallery](gallery.html)a link reading “see the gallery”
[[price to come]]a highlighted note, for anything still to fill in

Lists

Some boxes are lists, such as amenities or house rules. Use Add an item to add a line, drag the dotted handle beside an item to move it, and use the bin icon to delete it. Then Save.

Google description

Each page has a Google description. It's the short summary people see under the page's name in Google, and it isn't shown on the page itself. Keep it to one or two sentences.

Empty boxes: if you clear a box completely and save, that line disappears from the website. That's useful for removing a placeholder note.

Images

Changing photos

  1. Find the photo box you want to change. To clear the current photo, click its bin icon.
  2. Click Select to pick a photo that's already uploaded, or Upload to add a new one from your computer or phone.
  3. Click Save.

Upload the largest, original version you have. The website automatically shrinks big photos so pages stay fast, and it removes any hidden location data from them.

Photo descriptions

Many photos have a Photo description box. Write a short sentence about what's in the picture, for example “Long table under the pergola, laid for dinner”. It helps blind visitors using screen readers, and it helps Google.

Photo focus (advanced): a few homepage photos have this box. It controls how the photo is cropped. If you change one of those photos, empty this box too.

The Gallery

Adding, removing and reordering photos

Open Gallery, then Sections. You'll see The main house, Bedrooms, The pool house, Pool, Outside dining and Outside. Bedrooms has a sub-section for each bedroom.

Add a photo

  1. Open the section and click Add an item under Photos.
  2. Choose or upload the photo, and type a Caption. The caption shows when someone opens the photo full size.
  3. Click Save.

Remove a photo

Click the bin icon beside the photo (it says Remove item when you hover over it), confirm with Remove, then Save. The photo stays in your uploads, so you can add it back later.

Change the order

Drag photos up or down the list using the dotted handle beside each one, then Save. The list order is the order visitors click through when they open a photo full size.

Move a photo to another section

Dragging only works within one section. To move a photo to a different section:

  1. Open the section it should be in, click Add an item, then Select, and pick the photo from your uploads. Copy its caption across too.
  2. Open the section it was in by mistake, click the photo's bin icon and confirm with Remove.
  3. Click Save.

Removing a photo from a section never deletes the photo itself, so nothing is lost by moving things around.

Columns

On a computer, the gallery shows photos in three columns. Each photo has a Column setting:

  • Left, Centre or Right keeps the photo in that column. Photos in the same column run top to bottom in list order.
  • Blank lets the website place the photo in whichever column is shortest, so everything stays neat. This is the easiest choice.

On phones, all photos simply stack in list order.

A new section

Use Add an item under Sections, give it a Title, add photos and save. If a section has no photos yet, the website shows “Photographs to come”.

Booking page

Weeks and prices

Open Booking page, then Weeks. Each Saturday-to-Saturday week has a Status:

  • Available: shown normally, and guests can pick it on the enquiry form.
  • Booked: greyed out on the page, and removed from the enquiry form.

When a week is taken, change it to Booked and Save. To add a new week, use Add an item and fill in From and To.

Under Availability, the Prices from box sets the starting price, for example “€15,000 per week”. Leave it empty to show “to add”.

The Booking terms and House rules are here too. The terms are split into Sections (Payment, Security deposit, Tourist tax, Cancellation, Children), each with a title and bullet points. You can edit them, add a section or reorder them.

Figures to confirm are written in double brackets, like [[€2,500]], and show highlighted in yellow on the website. Replace each one with the final figure, without the brackets, and the highlight disappears. Make sure the terms match your rental contract exactly.

Your accounts

Who owns what

The website belongs to you. It's made up of four accounts, all in your name, and each does one job:

AccountWhat it doesWhat you do there
Pages CMSWhere you edit words, photos and weeks.Everyday editing, using this guide.
GitHubStores the website's files, with every saved version.Nothing day to day, but you use your GitHub login to sign in to Pages CMS. Pages CMS saves your changes here for you.
VercelPuts the website online, and republishes it about a minute after each change.Nothing day to day, apart from the monthly bill.
GoDaddyHolds your web address, casarossa.estate, and the settings for your casarossa.estate email.Renew the address each year.

Please don't change any DNS settings in GoDaddy without checking with Hillary first. Your email and your website both depend on them, and one wrong change can stop your email arriving.

Hillary's access

You never need to share a password. Hillary has her own access to GitHub and Pages CMS, so she can make bigger changes for you, and you can remove her access at any time.

Bigger changes

When to ask Hillary

Pages CMS covers words, photos, lists and weeks. Ask Hillary for anything that changes how the website is built:

  • Floor plans (they're drawings, so they can't be edited in Pages CMS)
  • New pages, or a different layout or design
  • Connecting or changing your web address (casarossa.estate)
  • Where enquiry and registration forms send their emails
  • Getting back an earlier version of the website

If something looks wrong

  • A change isn't showing: check you pressed Save, wait two minutes, then refresh the page. On a phone, close and reopen the page.
  • A photo looks blurry: upload a larger original.
  • The page layout looks broken: undo your last change and save. If that doesn't fix it, ask Hillary, who can restore the previous version.

Hillary has editing access to the same website, so she can step in on anything here at any time.
