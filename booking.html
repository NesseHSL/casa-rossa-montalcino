Booking terms sample text, to edit

Deposit. A deposit of 30% of the rental secures your week. The balance is due 60 days before arrival. A refundable security deposit of amount is also payable before arrival and returned within 14 days of departure.

Cancellations. If you need to cancel, we will refund payments made as follows:

  • More than 90 days before arrival: full refund, less a €250 administration fee
  • 60 to 90 days before arrival: 50% refund
  • Fewer than 60 days before arrival: no refund, unless we are able to re-let the week

We strongly recommend taking out travel insurance that covers cancellation.
