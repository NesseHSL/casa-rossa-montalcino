The Area

Brunello country, hill towns and the Tuscan coast, all within easy reach.

Location

In the heart of Brunello country

South of Siena in southern Tuscany, on the Castelgiocondo estate: 815 hectares of vineyards, forest and olive groves.

  • Castelgiocondo castle15 min walk
  • Montalcino15 min drive
  • Siena1 hr 15
  • Tyrrhenian coast1 hr 15
  • Florence1 hr 30
  • Florence airport1 hr 45
  • Pisa airport2 hr 30
  • Rome Fiumicino airport2 hr 45

For restaurants, shops and everyday essentials, Montalcino is just fifteen minutes away and Buonconvento just twenty. Over the years we have got to know the best tables in town and across the Val d'Orcia, and we are always happy to book them for you, from a long lunch among the vines to a special dinner out.
